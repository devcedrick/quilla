# Family 07 CSE preparation bank

270 original offline practice questions: 40 General Information, 50 Verbal, 60 Numerical, 80 Analytical and 40 Clerical. Each subject meets the 30/50/20 easy/medium/hard authoring targets; these labels estimate cognitive demand and are not empirical student success rates. Verbal has25 English and25 Filipino items.

Ten original PNG charts support the Analytical data questions. Each chart has an accessible exact-data description, versioned reference and byte-hashed asset metadata. Twenty-four dedicated Verbal references supply independently authored reading passages and paragraph parts. Canonical chart datasets live in scripts/family07-chart-data.json for reproducible rendering and independent calculations.

See the [manifest](manifest.json) and [review ledger](../../../docs/content/family07-review.md) for final inventory, sources, checks and remaining editorial gates. Analytical is Professional-only and Clerical Subprofessional-only; all Numerical items use shared-core eligibility. These are authoring batches, not fixed exams or official CSC topic weights.

Quilla is not affiliated with the Philippine Civil Service Commission. No actual or leaked examination questions are used. Answer-bearing content stays outside public assets and application imports. Human bilingual/domain review, empirical calibration and publication approval remain separate from offline validation.
