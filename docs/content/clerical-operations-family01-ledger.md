## Accuracy and originality safeguards

- Cc is **carbon copy**, rather than the reference's suggested expansion
  "copy furnished." The wording and behaviour were checked against
  [Microsoft's Cc guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Outlook/automatically-cc-carbon-copy-someone-on-every-email-you-send).
- Hidden Bcc addressing does not remove the message or attachment from what the
  Bcc recipient receives. The confidentiality task uses an explicit fictional
  office policy and a delegated handler, not a claim about legal requirements.
  See [Microsoft's Bcc guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/mail/show-hide-and-view-the-bcc-blind-carbon-copy-field-in-outlook-for-windows).
- Conventional fax scanning and transmission were checked against the
  [Brother sending-a-fax manual](https://download.brother.com/welcome/doc002488/mfc8880n_chneng_usr_a.pdf).
  The item does not claim that every contemporary fax service needs the same
  equipment or connection.
- CFO, treasurer, and auditor are assigned distinct responsibilities in the
  fictional directory. No universal equivalence of CFO and treasurer is asserted.
- Document-routing and acceptance rules are stated within the items. An invoice
  is not treated as universal proof of payment, and an order is not proof that
  its full quantity was accepted. These are document-control exercises, not
  Philippine tax, accounting, or legal advice.
- The document-classification item uses a defined dispatch-file boundary instead
  of asserting that a business can never hold an academic record.
- Source ideas are extended into distinct tasks rather than repeated with new
  names: recipient roles differ from delegated confidential routing; identifying
  hard copy differs from selecting a current physical copy; fax recognition,
  attempt logging, queue priority, and recovery test different operations.
- Names, organisations, callback details, records, and timestamps are fictional.
  Each item is self-contained, uses four distinct choices with one best answer,
  and explains why the main alternatives fail. Explanations do not depend on
  displayed option letters or positions. No question uses another item's answer.
