# Family 04 General and Verbal authoring review

Date: 2026-11-02. Original offline authorship; no import or publication. Quilla is not affiliated with the Philippine Civil Service Commission. See the pre-draft [coverage plan](family04-general-verbal-plan.md).

General40: 12easy/20medium/8hard. Verbal50:15/25/10; English25 (8/12/5), Filipino25 (7/13/5). Concepts: Vocabulary12, Grammar14, Reading12, Paragraph12. All 24 reading/paragraph items own dedicated independently authored references, each with one consumer.

## Evidence and review boundaries

Primary constitutional/statutory texts were retrieved 2026-11-02 from the Supreme Court E-Library and Lawphil. The precise provisions and source URLs appear below and in provenance. Legal statements are restricted to those provisions, not claims about every implementing rule or judicial exception. Hypothetical programmes, persons, cases and experiments are original. No officeholder/current-event trivia or actual CSE material is used.

English Merriam-Webster entries and Purdue agreement/tense text were retrieved. Filipino KWF masigásig and igtíng entries, Diksiyonaryo.ph masalimuot, aliwalas and the T-page containing tahas/tahak were retrieved. The KWF orthography PDF could not be opened in full: only the indexed NANG excerpt was available. DepEd ALS grammar/meaning competencies were retrieved; grammatical keys are independent sentence analyses, not an assertion that the portal itself validates each sentence. Exact PDF page verification and independent bilingual/domain editorial approval remain pending. The Purdue parallel-structure page was also inaccessible and was not used. The restrictive-clause/scope questions use independently stated meaning constraints; the specialised Purdue that/which page was subsequently retrieved through its grammar-index link.

## Per-item key, evidence and alternative analysis

Every item was solved as a separate task and each alternative checked in a distinct author review. Easy tasks identify explicit wording/sense. Medium tasks compare contextual interpretations or rule applications. Hard items combine constraints; paragraph distractors retain the opening so it alone cannot identify the key. Hard General08/15/16/23/24/31/32/40 explanations identify all rules rather than one familiar fact. Hard reading05/06 in each language distinguish supported claims, unknown results and unsupported extensions. Demand is editorial, not calibrated learner difficulty.
