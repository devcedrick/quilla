The medium revisions now require contextual distinctions, ordered mappings,
multiple spelling checks, partial-order possibilities, different rate
denominators, or state/operation tracking. Hard replacements require interacting
boundaries, truth-count constraints, enumeration of all admissible arrangements,
positive-integer feasibility, or interpreting several coupled propositions with
plausible partially correct alternatives. For example, Constitution 36 cannot be
answered by knowing only the succession threshold; its options also distinguish
the vote denominator and duration limit. Vocabulary English 03 and Reading
English 05 share correct sub-conclusions across options so one isolated fact
does not identify the answer.

These are reviewed cognitive-demand estimates under [rules.md](../../content/question-bank/rules.md),
not empirical calibration from student responses. Independent bilingual/domain
editorial approval and learner-response calibration remain pending; no reviewer
signature or publication approval is implied.

  times; item 14 explicitly chooses a qualifying listed pair, not all maxima.

  exclusive classification. Strengthened the medium shortcut examples as well.

  six by replacing item 06 with a cause/purpose distinction.

  and Clerical items. Revised explanations describe the actual error mechanisms.
- Checked revised tasks against Family 01 and searched the in-progress Family 02
  material for distinctive new scenarios/phrases. Shared words such as clock,
  anonymous, or receipt are not by themselves duplicates. No newly introduced
  substantive duplicate was identified; this is not a claim that automated exact
  matching proves originality across future families.
- Stored choice IDs are identities, not displayed letters. No unsupported shuffle
  field or forced answer-letter balancing was introduced.
