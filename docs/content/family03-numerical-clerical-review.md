Numerical coverage includes signed quantities, rounding, units and scale, prime factors, endpoint counting, area conversion, time boundaries, weighted means, similarity, residual fractions, profit bases, periodic alignment, simultaneous equations, no-replacement probability, inequalities, consumption, packing, inverse averages, minimax assignment, multiplicative change, conditional replacement, fee inequalities, conditional probability, mean/median/mode feasibility, and full digit-constraint solution sets. Clerical coverage includes date/code formats, completion routing, status legends, revision identification, acknowledgement scope, exact transcription, composite duplicate identifiers, eligibility-first queues, approved-version selection, serial identity, date rollover, exception routing, stock status, interval conflicts, deduplicated recipient unions, parallel indexing/scanning, approval authorization and double-booking, versioned ledger reconciliation, and incompatible-item packing. Sorting alternates letter-by-letter and word-by-word comparison, prefixes, name fields, punctuation, title/abbreviation normalization, and numeric tie breaks. Spelling uses 21 distinct target headwords with no target repeated between items.

Hard tasks require case distinctions or interacting constraints, not just uncommon terms or longer text. The minimax item proves a lower bound and constructs an attaining assignment. Conditional replacement splits mutually exclusive paths. Unique-mode feasibility excludes an otherwise smaller candidate that produces a tied mode. Pipeline and approval plans must satisfy every prerequisite and deadline. Parcel packing disproves the tempting aggregate capacity lower bound. Hard spelling audits require repairs and preservation decisions; all distractors retain some correct sub-decisions.

This is not a claim of independent human or psychometric approval.

Current tests independently calculate all 40 numerical outcomes; for bound intervals, full solution sets and time rollover they check semantic answers rather than unsafe number parsing.
